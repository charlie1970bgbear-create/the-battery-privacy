Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. About This Application

The Battery Restoration Manager is a locally operated application used to document cricket bat restorations, manage photographs, prepare sales listings and create draft listings for the application owner's eBay seller account.

2. Information Processed

The application processes information entered by its operator, including cricket bat specifications, restoration records, photographs, pricing and listing descriptions.

This information is stored locally on the operator's computer.

3. eBay Integration

With the operator's authorisation, the application may submit listing information and photographs to eBay to create draft listings.

The application is not intended to collect or manage buyer information, orders, payment details or customer communications.

Listings are reviewed, edited and published through eBay.

4. Authentication and Security

The application uses eBay's OAuth authorisation process. The operator's eBay password is not collected by The Battery Restoration Manager.

Authentication credentials required for the integration may be stored locally and should not be shared publicly.

5. Information Sharing

Listing information is transmitted to eBay only when the operator initiates an authorised listing-related action.

The application does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

6. Data Retention

Local application records remain on the operator's computer until deleted by the operator.

Authentication credentials and locally retained integration information may be removed by deleting the relevant local configuration. A dedicated disconnection function may be provided in a future application release.

7. Third-Party Services

When information is submitted to eBay, its processing is also governed by eBay's privacy policies.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application's functionality or data-handling practices.
